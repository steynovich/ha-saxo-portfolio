# The "Any" timezone mode polls at a fixed 15 minutes; market hours are regular weekday hours only

Besides the nine supported market timezones, users can choose "Any" (`DEFAULT_UPDATE_INTERVAL_ANY`, 15 min in `const.py`). It turns market-hours scheduling off: the coordinator never treats the market as open and polls every 15 minutes at all times. It exists for users whose markets are not listed or who want a predictable cadence, and 15 minutes sits between the 5 and 30 minute rates of [ADR-0004](0004-market-hours-aware-polling-and-rate-limiting.md), so it cannot be faster than the throttled market-hours rate. The spec's "5/30 minutes" (FR-007) therefore applies to the named timezones only.

Market hours are a static table (`MARKET_HOURS`) of regular weekday open and close times in the market's local time, DST-aware via `zoneinfo`. Exchange holidays, half days and lunch breaks (e.g. Tokyo, Hong Kong, Singapore) are not modelled, so on those days or hours the integration still polls at the 5 minute market-hours rate while the exchange is closed. The cost is some unneeded fast polls, still within the rate limits; a holiday calendar would add a dependency and upkeep for no meaningful gain.
